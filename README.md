# Secure Donations — 3D-Printable Donation Box

Open design files to build your own **contactless donation box** for the free
[Secure Donations](https://securedonations.net) app. Print the box, add a phone
running the app plus a Square reader, and you have a tap-to-give kiosk for your
mosque, church, temple, or community — funded into **your own Square account**,
with no cut taken from donations.

> **New here?** Get the app first — it's free:
> [**Get it on Google Play**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.securedonations.terminal)

## What's in this repo

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `Secure Donations Box/Base/Secure Donations Box Base.stl` | The main body of the box — holds the phone and reader, with the donation slot. |

## What else you'll need

- **A phone** running the Secure Donations app — the box is sized for the
  **Motorola Moto G Play (2024)**, available free on
  [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.securedonations.terminal).
- **A Square reader** — fits either model; the **2nd-generation** contactless +
  chip reader is recommended.
- **6 × magnets, 5 mm × 3 mm** — press-fit into the base and lid for a magnetic
  closure. *(Mind the polarity so the lid and base attract.)*
- **1 × steel rod, 3 mm × 180 mm** — pushed through the lid/base hinge to
  interlock the two halves.
- **PLA filament** — roughly **360 g** total (see below), plus a contrast color
  (ideally **gold**) for the logo.

Don't want to print and assemble it yourself? You can order a **Ready-Made Kit**
(everything included, pre-configured) or a **Provisioned Phone** at
[securedonations.net](https://securedonations.net).

## Printing

Material is **PLA** with your slicer's **default settings** — no special profile
needed.

| Part | Filament | Time (Bambu A1) | Supports |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Base | ~260 g | — | **None** |
| Lid (with logo) | just under 100 g | ~9 h 37 m | **Tree supports** |

### Printing the lid + logo (important)

The lid and both logo pieces are designed to print together as one multi-color
part:

1. Open/import **all three files in the `Lid` folder together** in your slicer.
2. When prompted **"Load these files as a single object with multiple parts?"**,
   click **Yes**.
3. In the **Prepare** tab, select the objects and set **Logo - 1** and
   **Logo - 2** to the same contrast color — **gold** looks best.
4. Enable **tree supports** (the design has some overhangs that print best with
   them).

The **base** prints on its own with **no supports**.

## Assembly

1. Print the **Base** (no supports) and the **Lid + logo** (tree supports, logo
   in gold).
2. Press the **six 5 × 3 mm magnets** into the recesses in the base and lid —
   check polarity so the lid closes and holds.
3. Push the **3 mm × 180 mm steel rod** through the aligned hinge on the lid and
   base to link them together.
4. Place the **Moto G Play (2024)** and your **Square reader** into the base.
5. Open the Secure Donations app, sign in, connect your Square account, and
   you're ready to collect.
